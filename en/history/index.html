A bar with history in Salta

In 2001, nightlife in Salta was almost entirely zambas and chacareras, the local folk music. That is when Zeppelin Rockería opened at Necochea 714, with an idea that seemed crazy at the time: a bar playing pure, uncompromising classic rock.

Twenty-five years, four addresses and thousands of nights later, Zeppelin is still where people from Salta celebrate their birthdays, meet up with friends and bring their visitors. It is no longer a rock bar: it is a bar of great music from every era, with signature cocktails made with northern Argentine ingredients and Terroir Extremo high-altitude wines. What never changed is the feeling of coming home.

2001
Necochea 714

The beginning: Zeppelin Rockería

It all started here. In a city of peñas and folk music, Zeppelin broke the mould with classic rock from start to finish. The bar found its identity and its crowd, and began writing its legend. One night in those years, Deep Purple was playing when a man walked in off the street, drawn by the music: it was Charly García, the legend of Argentine rock. He stayed, listened and ended up on stage.

2009
San Luis 428

Against all odds

Moving to the old red-light district sounded crazy. And yet something magical happened there: rock, indie and Britpop were playing, but people ended up singing cumbia. Zeppelin began to draw a wider, more diverse and freer crowd. These were the years of 80s, 90s, retro, Balkan and kitsch parties.

2012
Alvarado & Las Heras

The bridge

A necessary pause: seven months of transition that led Zeppelin to where it always belonged.

Today
25 de Mayo 340

The place you chose

Five blocks from Plaza 9 de Julio, in Salta’s historic centre. The walls are covered in messages written by the guests themselves: by you, by your friends, by everyone who came before. Generation after generation of locals have chosen this bar for their big nights. In November 2026, Zeppelin turned 25.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a historic bar to visit in Salta?

Yes. Zeppelin Bar, open since 2001, is one of the bars with the longest history in Salta’s nightlife. It started as Zeppelin Rockería at Necochea 714 and today is at 25 de Mayo 340, in the historic centre. Charly García, Molotov and Emanuel Horvilleur have all passed through its nights.

How long has Zeppelin Bar been open?

Since 2001. It turned 25 in November 2026.

Is it true that Charly García played at Zeppelin?

Yes. One night while Deep Purple was playing, Charly García walked in off the street drawn by the music, stayed and ended up on stage playing.

Is Zeppelin a rock bar?

It started as a rock bar in 2001, but today it is a bar of great music from every era and genre, with signature cocktails and high-altitude wines.

Where is it and when is it open?
