Why Zeppelin

Salta by day is the cathedral, the cable car and the Quebrada. Salta by night is Zeppelin. Opened in 2001 as Zeppelin Rockería, it was the first bar to bring rock to a city whose nightlife was all about folk music — and it has been part of local life ever since.

Today it is a bar of great music from every era, with walls covered in messages written by its own guests. Locals celebrate their birthdays here; travellers come for a drink after a peña and stay until late.

Getting there

On foot: 5 minutes from Plaza 9 de Julio — if you are staying in the city centre, Zeppelin is around the corner.

By taxi: just say “25 de Mayo 340, Salta Capital”. Under 10 minutes from anywhere in the centre.

Payment: cash, credit and debit cards, Mercado Pago and QR.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best bars in Salta, Argentina?

Salta’s nightlife combines folk music peñas on Balcarce Street, the Paseo Güemes bar strip and classic bars in the historic centre. Zeppelin Bar (25 de Mayo 340), open since 2001, is one of the most iconic: signature cocktails, high-altitude wines and great music, five blocks from Plaza 9 de Julio.

Where can I go out at night in Salta?

Start with dinner and a peña on Balcarce Street for live folk music, then head to a bar for drinks. Zeppelin Bar, in the city centre, is open Thursday to Saturday and on nights before public holidays from 8:30 pm, until 3 am on Thursdays and 5 am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Where can I try Salta wines at night?

Zeppelin Bar has a list of 30+ high-altitude wines from the Calchaquí Valleys (Terroir Extremo), grown between 1,700 and 2,685 m, including Torrontés and Malbec.

Is there a historic bar to visit in Salta?

Yes. Zeppelin Bar opened in 2001 as Zeppelin Rockería and is one of the bars with the longest history in Salta. Charly García, Molotov and Emanuel Horvilleur have all passed through its nights.

Do I need a reservation?

For groups of 6 or more, booking on WhatsApp (+54 9 387 545-8204) is recommended. Smaller groups can just walk in.
