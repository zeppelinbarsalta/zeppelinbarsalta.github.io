Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Salta at night?

Salta's nightlife combines folk music peñas on Balcarce Street, the Paseo Güemes restaurant strip and bars in the historic centre. Zeppelin Bar (25 de Mayo 340), open since 2001, serves signature cocktails, high-altitude wines and great music Thursday to Saturday and on holiday eves from 8:30 pm.

Is there nightlife in Salta on a Thursday?

Yes. Zeppelin Bar is open on Thursdays from 8:30 pm to 3 am, downtown, five blocks from Plaza 9 de Julio.

Where can I find a good cocktail bar in Salta?

Zeppelin Bar has signature cocktails made with ingredients from northwest Argentina (fig, cayote, quince, sweet potato) as well as the classics. It is at 25 de Mayo 340, Salta.

Is there a wine bar in Salta?

Zeppelin Bar has a Terroir Extremo high-altitude wine list with more than 30 labels from the Calchaquí Valleys (Cafayate, Cachi, Molinos), available by the glass or bottle at night in downtown Salta.

Peña first or bar first?

The peñas on Balcarce Street are best for dinner with a folk show. Afterwards, Zeppelin stays open until 5 am on Fridays and Saturdays and until 3 am on Thursdays, downtown.

Do I need a reservation at Zeppelin Bar?

Groups of 6 or more should book on WhatsApp (+54 9 387 545-8204). Smaller groups can just walk in.
