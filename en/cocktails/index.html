Frequently asked questions

Where can I get signature cocktails in Salta?

Zeppelin Bar (25 de Mayo 340, Salta) has a signature cocktail menu called "Sabores de Nuestra Tierra" (Flavours of Our Land), with 8 drinks made with regional ingredients from northwest Argentina. Open Thursday to Saturday from 8:30 pm.

Which regional ingredients are used in the cocktails?

Zeppelin’s signature menu uses fig, cayote, quince and sweet potato: traditional fruits and preserves of northwest Argentina that give each drink a flavour you won’t find in classic cocktails.

Which regional cocktails can I try in Salta?

Zeppelin’s signature drinks include Sombra de Higuera (Fig Old Fashioned), Cuesta del Obispo (Cayote Sour), Sangre Calchaquí (Quince Negroni) and Camino del Inca (Sweet Potato Cream Sour), among others.

Do you also serve classic cocktails?

Yes. Alongside the signature drinks there are classics like Mojito, Negroni, Old Fashioned, Aperol Spritz, Caipirinha, Pisco Sour and Margarita.
